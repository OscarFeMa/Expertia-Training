Item 6: Supersymmetry
- A theoretical framework suggesting a symmetry between particles with integer spin (bosons) and particles with half-integer spin (fermions).
- Proposes for every known particle, there exists a partner particle with different spin properties.
- Could explain phenomena like dark matter and the hierarchy problem in particle physics, but no experimental evidence has been found yet.
