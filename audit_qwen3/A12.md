**Item 4: Quantum Field Theory (QFT)**
- Combines quantum mechanics and special relativity to describe the behavior of elementary particles.
- Describes fundamental interactions through the exchange of force-carrying bosons.
- Successfully explains three of the four fundamental forces: electromagnetism, the weak nuclear force, and the strong nuclear force.
