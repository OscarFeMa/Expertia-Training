===Item 1: Quantum Mechanics===
- Quantum mechanics describes the behavior of matter and light at atomic and subatomic scales.
- It explains phenomena where classical physics fails, such as wave-particle duality and the uncertainty principle.
- Quantum mechanics underlies many fields, including chemistry, biology, and quantum information science.
