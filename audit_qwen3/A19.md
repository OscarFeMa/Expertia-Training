=== Item 1 ===
- Fred Hoyle (1915–2001) co-developed the B2FH paper (1957) with William A. Fowler and Robert B. Wilson, establishing the theory of stellar nucleosynthesis that explains how elements heavier than helium form inside stars.
- Hoyle controversially rejected the Big Bang theory in favor of a steady-state cosmological model (jokingly dubbed "the steady-state" on BBC Radio), later criticizing his own stance as a critique of inflationary cosmology.
