=== Item 4 ---
- Cytochrome P450 enzymes (CYPs) exhibit unique kinetic properties due to interactions with multiple substrates, deviating from standard hyperbolic saturation kinetics.
- These enzymes display a range of kinetic behaviors, including sigmoidal, biphasic, and substrate inhibition kinetics.
- Models and equations explaining these atypical kinetic profiles will be discussed.
