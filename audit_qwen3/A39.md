1. Chromatography:
   - A laboratory technique used to separate a mixture into its components based on differences in the affinity of each component for a stationary phase and a mobile phase.
   - It can be preparative (to purify components for later use) or analytical (to measure the presence or proportions of analytes in a mixture).
   - Subtle differences in a compound's partition coefficient lead to differential retention on the stationary phase and affect the separation.
